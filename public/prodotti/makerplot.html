Description

MakerPlot is a fully 3D printable Arduino CNC plotter designed for makers, students and electronics enthusiasts. This DIY kit allows you to build your own desktop drawing machine using 3D printed parts, Arduino electronics and simple mechanical components. The project has been designed to be easy to assemble while teaching the fundamentals of CNC machines, motion control and digital fabrication. The download includes all the STL files required to print the complete structure of the machine.
